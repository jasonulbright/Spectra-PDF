# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person you're replying to is acting in good faith and
knows things you don't.

## What's expected

- Keep criticism on the work, not the person.
- Accept that the maintainer may say no. This project has a specific direction
  and a high completeness bar; a declined suggestion is a scoping decision, not
  a judgement of you.
- Be patient. This is one person's project, and issues may sit for a while.
- Assume varying expertise. "Have you tried X" is fine; condescension is not.

## What isn't

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or demeaning remarks, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing someone's private information,
or unwelcome sexual attention. Any of these will get comments removed and, if
repeated, the account blocked from the repository.

## Reporting

Report a problem by opening an issue, or -- if it involves the content of a
public thread or you'd rather not do it publicly -- by contacting the
maintainer through the repository's security advisory form, which is private.
Reports will be handled discreetly.

This is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
kept short deliberately: a document nobody reads protects nobody.
